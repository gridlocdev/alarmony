# Alarmony

A clock app for your desktop and your browser: timers, a stopwatch, alarms and a world clock, plus an `alarmony` command for the terminal. Everything lives in one plain `clock.json` in a folder you choose, so you can back it up, sync it, or read it with other tools.

![The Timers tab: three timer cards in a row (Tea with 20 seconds left and its ring nearly empty, Pasta paused at 11:20 and selected, Bread at 1:29:20 with its ring full), each with Cancel and Pause or Resume buttons. Under them, the dashed New Timer card offers a Focus preset of 25:00, buttons for 1, 3, 5 and 10 minutes, and Custom…](docs/images/timers.webp)

- **Alarms and timers** that ring until you stop them, with snooze, repeat days and one-click presets. On the desktop they keep ringing from the menu bar after the window closes.
- **Stopwatch and world clock**: laps with the fastest and slowest marked, and places by city name with their offset from you.
- **Keyboard first**: every action is a command in the ⌘K menu and on right-click, with shortcuts listed under ⌘/. Undo takes back anything, including changes made in another window or from the command line.

## Getting started

Download the desktop app for macOS, Windows or Linux from [Releases](https://github.com/gridlocdev/alarmony/releases/latest). [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md) covers running it from source, the command line and where your data is kept.
