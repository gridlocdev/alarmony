# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A stopwatch with laps, and countdown timers with labels and presets, in the browser and as a desktop app.
- A finished timer rings until you stop it, on every running copy that has Ring on this device turned on. One missed while Alarmony was closed shows as a notice.
- Everything is saved to one `clock.json`: in Documents/Alarmony on the desktop, or in the browser's own storage.
- Every action is in the ⌘K command menu, with the shortcuts under ⌘/. Undo and redo take back any change.
- Light and dark themes, and a 12- or 24-hour clock.
